Immigration for the two-year transfer

Work visa

Apply for a Global Business Mobility Senior or Specialist Worker visa if the job qualifies. The UK subsidiary needs a licence for this route and must issue a Certificate of Sponsorship (CoS).

{% include "estimator/source_links.html" with references=guidance.route only %}

Business visit

For meetings or site visits as a Standard Visitor, a Swiss passport holder normally needs an electronic travel authorisation (ETA) instead of a visa. It costs £{{ fees.eta }}. Allow up to three working days for approval before travelling.

{% include "estimator/source_links.html" with references=guidance.visit only %}

Passport

Use a Swiss passport valid for the whole stay. Travel with the passport used for the ETA. A replacement passport needs a new ETA. For the assignment, keep your current passport linked to your UK Visas and Immigration account.

{% include "estimator/source_links.html" with references=guidance.passport only %}

Work visa requirements

  • Job and pay. The job must be eligible. Annual pay must meet £52,500 or the rate for the job, whichever is higher. CHF 150,000 is about £136,364 at the pack’s exchange rate. The sponsor must check the job code, hours and pay before assigning the certificate. {% include "estimator/source_links.html" with references=guidance.eligibility only %}
  • Overseas employment. The worker must be 18 or older and already employed by the group. Below £73,900 a year, the worker needs 12 months of qualifying work overseas. The pack gives three years with the Swiss company. {% include "estimator/source_links.html" with references=guidance.overseas_service only %}
  • Money for arrival. Show £1,270 held for 28 consecutive days. The last day must fall within 31 days of applying. An A-rated sponsor can certify support on the certificate instead. {% include "estimator/source_links.html" with references=guidance.visa_fees only %}

Processing times

New Worker sponsor licence
Most take less than eight weeks. If available, priority costs £750 for a decision within ten working days. {% include "estimator/source_links.html" with references=guidance.licence only %}
Extra certificates
If the sponsor needs more certificates, the request usually takes up to 12 weeks. {% include "estimator/source_links.html" with references=guidance.certificate only %}
Work visa from Switzerland
Usually three weeks after the worker applies, proves their identity and sends the documents. If offered, priority costs £500 and usually takes up to five working days. {% include "estimator/source_links.html" with references=guidance.visa_timing only %}

Allow about 11 weeks for a new licence followed by a standard visa. Leave extra time for any appointments or extra certificates.

Employer steps

  1. Check the licence and job. Confirm the group link, job code and pay. Apply for a licence or add the route if the subsidiary needs it. {% include "estimator/source_links.html" with references=guidance.route only %}
  2. Assign the certificate. Pay the sponsor fees and give the worker the reference. The worker applies from Switzerland with their passport, certificate reference and job details. Apply within three months of getting the certificate and no more than three months before the job starts. {% include "estimator/source_links.html" with references=guidance.work_application only %}
  3. Check right to work before the start date. Use the worker’s share code and date of birth. Start work only once the work visa is valid. Keep the result, repeat the check when required and report changes as a sponsor. {% include "estimator/source_links.html" with references=guidance.right_to_work only %}
