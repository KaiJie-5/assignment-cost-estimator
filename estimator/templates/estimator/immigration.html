Immigration for the Zurich to London assignment

One Swiss worker on a two-year transfer. Fees checked 24 November 2026.

Work visa and eligibility

The route for this transfer is the Global Business Mobility Senior or Specialist Worker visa, provided the role and sponsor qualify. The worker must be at least 18 and already work for the linked group. The UK subsidiary needs a Worker sponsor licence covering this route and must assign a Certificate of Sponsorship (CoS).

{% include "estimator/source_links.html" with references=guidance.route only %}
  • Role and pay. Pay must be at least £52,500 a year or the occupation going rate, whichever is higher. The pack’s CHF 150,000 base salary is about £136,364 at its exchange rate, above the general pay floor. The sponsor still needs to check the eligible occupation code and compare qualifying pay with the going rate for the actual hours. {% include "estimator/source_links.html" with references=guidance.eligibility only %}
  • Overseas service. The worker’s three years with the Swiss company meet the 12-month requirement if that service qualifies. The minimum overseas period is waived for qualifying pay of £73,900 or more. {% include "estimator/source_links.html" with references=guidance.overseas_service only %}
  • Maintenance. For this application from Switzerland, show £1,270 held for 28 days ending within 31 days of applying, unless an A-rated sponsor certifies maintenance on the CoS. {% include "estimator/source_links.html" with references=guidance.visa_fees only %}

Business visit before the assignment

For this business trip, the worker can travel as a Standard Visitor with an ETA in place of a visitor visa. The ETA costs £{{ fees.eta }}. Allow up to three working days and wait for approval before travel. Travellers who already hold valid UK immigration permission can use that permission.

{% include "estimator/source_links.html" with references=guidance.visit only %}

Use the visit for permitted activities such as meetings or site visits. Return to Switzerland to apply for the work visa and start the UK role once work permission is in force.

{% include "estimator/source_links.html" with references=guidance.visit_activities only %}

Passport and eVisa

Use a Swiss passport valid throughout each stay. Travel for the visit with the passport used for the ETA. A replacement passport needs a new ETA if one is still required.

{% include "estimator/source_links.html" with references=guidance.passport only %}

For the work application, provide the passport, CoS reference, and the job and sponsor details. After approval, link the passport you will travel with to your UKVI account and update the account after a renewal.

Processing times

New Worker sponsor licence
Most applications take less than eight weeks. For a new licence and then a standard work visa, budget about 11 weeks for the two decisions, plus time for documents, appointments and any extra checks. Licence priority is £750 for a decision within ten working days if available. {% include "estimator/source_links.html" with references=guidance.licence only %}
Certificate allocation
A sponsor with the correct licence can assign a CoS from its allocation. If it needs more certificates, the request usually takes up to 12 weeks. {% include "estimator/source_links.html" with references=guidance.certificate only %}
Work visa application from Switzerland
Usually three weeks after the worker has applied, proved their identity and sent the documents. If offered, £500 priority usually brings the decision within five working days. {% include "estimator/source_links.html" with references=guidance.visa_timing only %}

Employer steps

  1. Confirm the transfer. Document the group link, overseas employment, UK job code and pay. Check that the subsidiary’s licence covers this route. Apply for a licence or add the route if needed. {% include "estimator/source_links.html" with references=guidance.route only %}
  2. Assign the CoS and pay the sponsor fees. Give the worker the reference to apply within three months of assignment and at most three months before the job start date. The sponsor pays the certificate fee and any Immigration Skills Charge due. {% include "estimator/source_links.html" with references=guidance.certificate only %} {% include "estimator/source_links.html" with references=guidance.skills_charge only %}
  3. Check permission to work before the start date. Use the worker’s share code and date of birth for the online check, keep the evidence and repeat the check when required. Maintain sponsor records and report relevant changes. {% include "estimator/source_links.html" with references=guidance.right_to_work only %}
